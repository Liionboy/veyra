# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Veyra welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Protect private information and security-report details.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized behavior, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Report
conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and fairly.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
